# Fit Scoring Rubric

100 points across six dimensions. Score each independently, then sum. Every
dimension needs a one-line evidence citation.

Apply this rubric only after every eligibility gate has passed.

## Mission alignment — 30 points

How closely the funder's stated purpose matches the organization's mission.

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 26–30 | Funder's stated priority names this exact issue area and population |
| 19–25 | Clear overlap; org's work is a recognizable instance of what they fund |
| 11–18 | Adjacent — plausible but requires framing work to connect |
| 4–10 | Tangential; the connection would strain credibility |
| 0–3 | No meaningful overlap |

Evidence: quote the funder's own priority language. Do not score from the grant
title alone — titles are broad and misleading.

## Program fit — 25 points

Whether the specific project matches what this funder actually buys.

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 21–25 | Funder has funded near-identical projects; activity type explicitly eligible |
| 15–20 | Activity type eligible, project is a normal example of it |
| 8–14 | Eligible but atypical for this funder |
| 3–7 | Arguably eligible, no precedent in their giving history |
| 0–2 | Activity type excluded |

Evidence: cite specific prior grants from the 990-PF or the funder's grants
list. Watch for exclusions — capital, equipment, staff salaries, indirect costs,
and general operating support are each commonly excluded.

## Funding size fit — 15 points

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 13–15 | Request sits mid-range of their typical grant size |
| 9–12 | Within range, near an edge |
| 5–8 | At the extreme of their range |
| 2–4 | Outside typical range but not formally capped |
| 0–1 | Above the cap, or so far below that the effort is not worth it |

Derive typical grant size from the 990-PF grants paid schedule — the median of
actual grants, not the maximum advertised. Funders advertise "up to $250,000"
and routinely award $25,000.

## Geographic fit — 10 points

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Org and beneficiaries sit squarely in the funder's priority area |
| 6–8 | Within the eligible area, not a priority sub-area |
| 3–5 | Partially within; some beneficiaries outside |
| 0–2 | Mostly outside (a hard exclusion is a gate, not a score) |

## Capacity to comply — 10 points

Whether the org can carry the reporting, financial, and administrative load.

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Requirements at or below what the org already does routinely |
| 6–8 | Modest lift — one new report or tracking process |
| 3–5 | Significant lift — new financial systems, evaluation capacity, or audit |
| 0–2 | Beyond current capacity; accepting would strain the organization |

Score honestly. An award the org cannot administer costs more than it brings in,
and damages the funder relationship for future asks.

## Funder relationship & history — 10 points

| Points | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Current or recent funder in good standing |
| 7–8 | Past funder, or a warm introduction is available |
| 4–6 | No relationship, but the funder accepts unsolicited proposals openly |
| 2–3 | No relationship, and the funder is known to favor existing grantees |
| 0–1 | Prior rejection with no material change since |

Check `grants/outcomes.md` for prior history with this funder before scoring.

## Effort estimate

Score does not capture cost. Alongside the score, estimate effort so the user
can weigh return:

- **Light** — under 8 hours. Short form, existing narratives cover it.
- **Moderate** — 1–3 days. Custom narrative, standard attachments, budget.
- **Heavy** — 1–2 weeks. Logic model, letters of support, detailed evaluation
  plan, multi-year budget, partner commitments.
- **Very heavy** — 3+ weeks. Federal applications, SF-424 family, indirect cost
  negotiation, registrations in SAM.gov.

A 70-point light-effort opportunity often beats an 85-point very-heavy one,
especially for a small team. Say so when it applies.

## Confidence

Note confidence separately from score:

- **High** — funder publishes guidelines and a grants list; both were read.
- **Medium** — guidelines read, giving history inferred from 990-PF.
- **Low** — sparse public information; score is an estimate.

Low confidence on a high score means "call the program officer", not "apply".
